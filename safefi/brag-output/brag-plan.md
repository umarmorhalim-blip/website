# /brag plan — SafeFi+ "Journey of a Transfer"

**What it is:** a scroll-driven 3D landing page that follows one SafeFi+ transfer (RM → USDT) from the app to settlement.
**For whom:** Malaysians new to crypto who care about safety and Shariah compliance more than speculation.
**What sets it apart:** it doesn't describe the process, it shows it: the money is a glowing packet you can follow through every check.
**Visual hook:** the transfer as a packet of light flying through a verification ring that turns green.
**Share caption:** "Where does your money go after you tap Confirm? We built a page that follows it."

**Angle:** trust through visibility. Every step a newcomer worries about (who checks me? is it halal? who approves it? where is it now?) gets its own beat, in the site's own words.
**Tone:** default (clean, punchy) leaning polished; it's money, so no jokes.
**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 22.5 s.
**Identity:** the site itself: ink #07050F, purple #A855F7 / #C084FC / #7C3AED, gold #D4A853, green #4ADE80, Plus Jakarta Sans.
**Source:** the real page, rendered frame by frame (`?capture` mode). Nothing rebuilt; the cards, 3D scene and copy are the production components.

| 9 | 19.0–22.5 | **Punchline / outro** | SafeFi+ mark, "Shariah-compliant USDT ↔ MYR", Google Play · app.safefiplus.com. |

## Sound
Warm D-major bed at 96 BPM (pad, soft pluck arpeggio, round bass, gentle kick from the reveal). Effects pitched in key and mixed under the music: a soft tick on "Confirm", airy swells on camera moves, a bell when the ring turns green, eight rising ticks for the votes, a low lock thump, a bright chord on "Settled".

## Notes
Sample figures (RM 1,000 → 242.72 USDT, 8 validators) are the site's illustrative examples and stay labelled as such on screen.
