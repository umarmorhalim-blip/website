01 Our story

Why a governance firm moved into blockchain

Qawwam Empire Sdn Bhd (QESB) was set up in 2022 with a simple aim: a professional services firm that is good at the work and principled in how it does it.

We began as corporate advisers — governance, compliance and organisational development. That work taught us, in detail, how well-governed organisations run.

As the digital economy grew, we took that discipline into blockchain and digital-asset advisory, a field where weak governance is expensive and common.

Today we work with corporations, institutions and individuals in Malaysia and abroad, as advisers, trainers and, through SafeFi+, as a platform operator. Our digital-asset activities sit with Qawwam Digital Inc., our Labuan-regulated subsidiary.

Company at a glance

  1. 2022

    QESB incorporated; corporate advisory begins in Bandar Baru Bangi, Selangor.

  2. 2023

    Expands into blockchain and digital-asset consultancy.

  3. 2024

  4. 2025

    Passes 10,000 clients across professional and digital services.

  5. 2026

    Launches SafeFi+, our own blockchain and digital-asset platform. In July, appoints BIMB Securities as Shariah Adviser.

06 Governance

How we are governed

Governance is not a box we tick for regulators. It is how decisions get made here.

Corporate governance

01

Board Charter

02

Independent oversight

Compliance & ethics

04

AML/CFT framework
