01 Our history

Why a governance firm moved into blockchain

Qawwam Empire Sdn Bhd (QESB) was established in 2022 to provide professional services delivered with technical rigour and integrity.

We began as corporate advisers in governance, compliance and organisational development — work that gave us detailed insight into how well-governed organisations operate.

In 2023 we extended that discipline to blockchain and digital-asset advisory, a field in which strong governance is essential.

Today we serve corporations, institutions and individuals in Malaysia and abroad as advisers and trainers, and through the SafeFi+ platform. Our digital-asset activities are carried out by Qawwam Digital Inc., our Labuan-regulated subsidiary.

Milestones

  1. 2022

    QESB incorporated; corporate advisory begins in Bandar Baru Bangi, Selangor.

  2. 2023

    Expands into blockchain and digital-asset consultancy.

  3. 2024

    Surpasses 1,000 active clients; broadens compliance and governance advisory.

  4. 2025

    Surpasses 10,000 clients across professional and digital services.

  5. 2026

    Launches the SafeFi+ digital-asset platform. In July, appoints BIMB Securities Sdn Bhd as Shariah Adviser.
