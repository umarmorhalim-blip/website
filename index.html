01 About the company

A governance firm first. Digital assets second.

QESB started in 2022 as a corporate advisory and consultancy practice, working on governance, compliance and organisational development. That gave us a close view of how well-run organisations actually operate.

06 Questions

Common questions

What does QESB actually do?

Three things. We advise organisations on blockchain, digital assets, cybersecurity and regulatory compliance. We run training programmes in those same areas. And we build and operate SafeFi+, our own digital-asset platform.

Are your training programmes HRDC-approved?

Yes. Our training modules are HRDC-approved, so eligible Malaysian employers can fund them through their HRD Corp levy. Tell us your headcount and objectives and we will propose a programme.

What does “Shariah-compliant” mean in practice?

Our appointed Shariah Adviser, BIMB Securities Sdn Bhd, reviews and validates our products, contracts and operating frameworks, so that what we recommend and build stays within Shariah principles.

Which regulations do you advise on?

Mainly the frameworks of Bank Negara Malaysia, the Securities Commission and the Labuan FSA for digital assets, AML/CFT obligations, and the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 for data protection.

How quickly will you respond to an enquiry?

Have a project or a compliance question?

Tell us where you are and what you need. We reply to every enquiry within two business days.
