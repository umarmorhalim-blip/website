01 About the company

Governance expertise, applied to digital assets.

QESB was established in 2022 in Bandar Baru Bangi, Selangor, as a corporate advisory practice focused on governance, compliance and organisational development — work that gave us close insight into how well-run organisations operate.

In 2023 we extended our practice to blockchain and digital-asset advisory, applying the same compliance discipline to a fast-developing field.

06 Questions

Frequently asked questions

What services does QESB provide?

QESB provides three services: advisory on blockchain, digital assets, cybersecurity and regulatory compliance; training programmes in the same areas; and SafeFi+, our digital-asset platform.

Are your training programmes HRD Corp-approved?

Yes. Our training modules are HRD Corp-approved, so eligible employers can fund them through their HRD Corp levy. Share your participant numbers and objectives and we will propose a programme.

What does “Shariah-compliant” mean in practice?

Our Shariah Adviser, BIMB Securities Sdn Bhd, reviews our products, contracts and operating frameworks to ensure they are consistent with Shariah principles.

Which regulatory frameworks do you advise on?

Our advisory covers frameworks issued by Bank Negara Malaysia, the Securities Commission Malaysia and the Labuan Financial Services Authority on digital assets and AML/CFT, as well as the Personal Data Protection Act 2010. Our advisory does not constitute legal advice.

How quickly will you respond to an enquiry?

We aim to respond within two business days. Please use the enquiry form, email info@qawwamempire.com or call +603-8928 0612 during office hours.

Have a project or a compliance question?
