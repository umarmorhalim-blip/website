About Us

Governance with Purpose

Founded in 2022, Qawwam Empire Sdn Bhd (QESB) was established with a clear purpose — to create a professional services organisation that is not only operationally excellent, but ethically grounded in every dimension.

We began as a corporate advisory and consultancy firm, building our foundations on governance, compliance and organisational development. This early focus gave us a distinct advantage: a deep, institutional understanding of how well-governed organisations operate — an understanding that now underpins everything we do.
