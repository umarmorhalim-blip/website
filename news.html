Qawwam Empire appoints BIMB Securities as official Shariah Adviser

Qawwam Empire Sdn Bhd has appointed BIMB Securities Sdn Bhd as its designated Shariah Adviser.

The Acknowledgement of Appointment was signed by Nik Muhammad Rafie Nik Zainal Alam, Managing Director of Qawwam Empire, and Dr. Rosnani Rasul, CEO and Executive Director of BIMB Securities.

Under the engagement, BIMB Securities will review and validate Qawwam Empire’s products, contracts and operating frameworks for compliance with Shariah principles, and advise on Islamic finance matters as the company expands.
