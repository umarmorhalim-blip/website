Qawwam Empire Sdn Bhd appoints BIMB Securities Sdn Bhd as Shariah Adviser

BANDAR BARU BANGI, 22 July 2026 – Qawwam Empire Sdn Bhd (QESB) has appointed BIMB Securities Sdn Bhd as its Shariah Adviser.

The Acknowledgement of Appointment was signed by Mr. Nik Muhammad Rafie bin Nik Zainal Alam, Founder and Managing Director of QESB, and Dr. Rosnani binti Rasul, Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director of BIMB Securities Sdn Bhd.

Under the engagement, BIMB Securities will review and validate QESB’s products, contracts and operating frameworks to ensure compliance with Shariah principles, and provide guidance on Islamic finance matters to support the company’s growth.

About Qawwam Empire Sdn Bhd

Qawwam Empire Sdn Bhd (QESB) is a corporate advisory firm based in Bandar Baru Bangi, Selangor. Established in 2022, QESB advises organisations on blockchain, digital-asset and cybersecurity governance, delivers HRD Corp-approved training and operates the SafeFi+ digital-asset platform. BIMB Securities Sdn Bhd is QESB’s appointed Shariah Adviser.
